WireChat redesign directions

Three retro directions, then three new layouts for the web and mobile apps, built from the real screens. Open any board to see it full size. Boards with a "Switch theme" button flip between dark and light.

Design mockups only. Nothing here changes how the app works.

Courrier new layout

Inbox layout. Servers are tabs across the top, one list holds every channel with a preview of its last message, and the conversation opens in a reading pane. Serif headings and thin rules, in the WireChat palette.

Bento new layout

A floating dock and rounded tiles. Home is a dashboard, messages show as bubbles with channel pills above them, and each settings tile holds its own control. WireChat browns with one amber hero tile.

Standard new layout

Switchboard layout. Open several channels side by side as columns, with a patch panel of every line and a command bar. Dense and keyboard-first, amber on WireChat brown. Themes are in the next row.

Standard · themes new row

Six themes for the Standard layout: a highlight colour (amber, givre #B2E9EB, CRT) in dark or light. Backgrounds stay WireChat brown or cream; only the highlight changes. In the CRT themes, the screen effect (scanlines, glow, flicker) only shows on the item you click. Mobile: ambre sombre · ambre clair · givre sombre · givre clair · crt sombre · crt clair

Pavage new layout

Tiling window manager. Each server is a workspace in the top bar, and channels, members, friends and settings are tiled windows with gaps. The focused window glows amber, while dialogs and the reaction picker float above. Keyboard-first, in the WireChat palette.

Zinzin new layout

Goofy corkboard. Servers are fridge magnets, panels are tilted sticky notes, the conversation is a detective wall with red string, settings orbit your avatar like a solar system, and members are a hand of trading cards. Googly eyes follow the cursor. WireChat palette, motion off with reduced motion.

Détraqué new layout

Freaky VHS horror. A self-scrolling server spine, a giant eye that follows the cursor, shattered glass panels, a composer inside a mouth with teeth, eye toggles, missing-person posters for members and a pulsing delete button that asks three times. WireChat brown and amber with a red/ice chromatic split. Web only.

Velours new layout

Late-night lounge. Burgundy velvet, satin, lipstick red and gold, high-fashion italic serif and a flickering neon sign. Servers are a pearl necklace, settings sit around a dressing-room mirror, and members are on a VIP list behind a velvet rope. WireChat brown and amber at the base. Web only.
